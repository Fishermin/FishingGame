# Field Guide — freshwater roster

Companion to [`GAME_DESIGN.md`](GAME_DESIGN.md). Species are North American
inland fish. Names match what an angler would say on the bank (the trip
game's "Sheepshead" and "Dogfish" and "Eelpout" stay as primary names;
scientific or textbook names are aliases).

Starter three are marked. The original twenty trip-game species are marked
with a dagger (†). River monsters are last and are not real species.

Rarity: C common · U uncommon · R rare · T trophy · L legend.

Count: **74 regular species + 5 river monsters = 79**.

## Encounter design notes

- Cut bait still boosts catfish, musky, sturgeon, pike — same list as the
  trip game — and still risks a zero-bite.
- Worms weight Sun and Stone commons. **Crawler Finder** can supply worms
  from the overworld after rain.
- Minnows weight Fang.
- Leeches weight Night and Cold.
- Named lures weight Swift and Weed depending on color (firetiger, etc.).
- Tragedy at the Dam Store removes Brook Trout and Rainbow Trout from
  Englehorn / Granite Falls until that arc is resolved.
- Ice replaces open-water tables with smelt, burbot, lake trout, pike.
